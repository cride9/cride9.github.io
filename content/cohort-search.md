## Research context
My current university thesis explores clinical document retrieval and cohort discovery. The focus here is the engineering architecture, not clinical advice or claims of clinical validation.

## System design
Hybrid structured and semantic search supports inclusion and exclusion criteria. Agentic retrieval helps investigate relevant evidence across clinical documents.

## Traceability
Evidence verification, traceable results, and audit trails are central system concerns. A result should remain connected to the material that supports it.

## Privacy-aware engineering
Local medical language models and document processing are considered within a privacy-aware architecture. This is the newest growth in my work: applying retrieval infrastructure to research-oriented systems.
