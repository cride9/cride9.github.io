## Context
Reverse engineering was an early route into understanding how compiled software behaves beyond its documented interfaces.

## My contribution
I developed a CS:GO internal mod menu with native C++ and ImGui, including custom Radio1/audio functionality. The work involved runtime modification, binary analysis, Windows internals, and hooking concepts.

## Engineering focus
The challenge was connecting low-level execution to usable tooling: investigating behavior, reasoning about process state, and debugging native integrations.

## What it led to
That work provided a foundation for native integration at FyreZone and a continuing interest in the layers beneath high-level APIs.
