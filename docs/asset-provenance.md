# Asset provenance

The tree geometry, bark maps, and fallback renders are original assets created for this portfolio by the checked-in Blender/Python authoring tools. No stock tree, external texture pack, or generated photograph is used. The user supplied CGTrader's [Winter Tree 2 by background-assets](https://www.cgtrader.com/free-3d-models/plant/other/winter-tree-2) as a shape reference: serpentine trunk, lateral limbs, irregular terminal branching. The CGTrader model was not downloaded or redistributed; its download required account login. Snow was not carried into the monochrome wood material.

- Editable source: `assets-source/living-tree.blend`, Blender 4.5.0.
- Geometry: original authored primary paths with seeded secondary branching; seed 7319.
- Texture maps: original tileable mathematical bark, generated by `tools/create-bark.py`.
- Runtime compression: Meshopt and Basis Universal UASTC, with bundled Three.js Basis decoder.
- Fonts: DM Sans and Manrope, Google Fonts / SIL Open Font License.
- Basis transcoder: upstream Three.js redistribution from Binomial's Basis Universal, Apache-2.0. Preserve the copyright/license notice in the distributed JS and copy the upstream license alongside it.
- Icons: simple text glyphs and an original geometric favicon.

Coordinate contract: Y-up, metres, nominal 12.6 m height. Individual semantic meshes retain `_U` and `_PATHINDEX`. Do not quantize vertex positions without also transforming the spline atlas; normal-only quantization is intentional.
