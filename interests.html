My Interests

Mechanical Keyboards

I love modding keyboards. I have collections of switches like Outemu Blues, Gateron Yellow Silents (my current daily drivers), and Akko Jelly Purples. I've also modded and tuned keyboards for friends, from lubing switches to tuning stabilizers.

Beatboxing

I started learning because of NaPoM and I'm a fanatic of the liproll sound. I can do inward/outward bass, normal liprolls, and I'm currently learning vocal liprolling. While I'm better at copying famous covers than creating my own beats, it's a super fun skill to practice.

Music Listening

Anything that is Japanese! I listen to a huge variety, including Metal, J-Pop, and Vocaloid. Playing a lot of Osu! has definitely shaped my music taste.

Gaming

I'm a big Osu! player, currently ranked around 29k globally (see my profile). My long-term goal is to pass "Dance number o tomo ni" with a B score. I also love MMORPGs and story-driven games in wilderness settings, like Far Cry Primal and Horizon Zero Dawn. Black Desert Online is my go-to MMORPG, with nearly 10k hours invested.
